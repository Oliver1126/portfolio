Where I plug in

Every project starts with a bottleneck.

Here's what I actually noticed, and what I built to fix it.

01
The bottleneck

Recruiters lose hours manually screening CVs

Every batch of resumes gets read one by one, with no consistent way to judge a fit or explain why a candidate was ranked the way they were.

Fixed with Nexu AI — screens and ranks a stack of CVs against a job in minutes, with a written reason for every score.
02
The bottleneck

Lead and candidate pipelines get lost across spreadsheets and email

No single place to see what's new, what's scored, and what needs a human decision, so good leads go cold.

Fixed with the Ops Dashboard — captures leads and uses AI to score and organize the pipeline, with humans approving the calls that matter.
03
The bottleneck

Planning an event means juggling five apps and a group chat

Guest lists in one place, budget in a spreadsheet, suppliers found through word of mouth, and no single view of the day.

Fixed with PlanSync PH — one app for guests, budget, RSVPs, suppliers, and an AI speech writer. Shipped to the Play Store.
04
The bottleneck

AI agents are unpredictable and hard to trust with real decisions

Most automation is a black box: it works until it doesn't, and nobody can tell you why.

Fixed by how I run NodalNexus — every AI project is delivered in small, auditable stages, so every result comes with the reasoning behind it.

Why I built it
